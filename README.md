# Handover: move-out marketplace pretotype

Pretotype website for a university group project (Digital Entrepreneurship & AI module).
Handover helps students leaving Dublin clear their room fast, with drop-off collection days
or a pickup, and lets arriving students buy single items or starter bundles.

**This is a prototype, not a live service.** Nothing on the page collects data, and all dates,
prices and residences are examples. It is not affiliated with any college or accommodation provider.

- `site/index.html`: the whole website (single static file, no build step)
- `AI_LOG.md`: record of AI-generated content, what we accepted or changed, and why

To view locally, open `site/index.html` in a browser.
