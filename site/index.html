How it works · moving out

Clear your room in four steps

You don't need to know where to sell, or wait around for buyers. Handover sells it for you, and you can leave on time.

Snap photos

Take a few photos of each thing you can't take home. One photo per item is enough.

We draft the listing

Handover suggests a title, condition and fair price. Change anything you like, then publish.

The bit that matters

Hand it over

Bring everything to your residence's collection day, or book a pickup before you fly. Then you're done.

Get paid

Your things sell one by one, and anything left over can go into a starter bundle for arriving students. You get 80% of the price, paid to your Revolut or bank account, even after you've left Ireland.

Questions

Good to know

I'm leaving in three days. Is that too late?

No. List tonight, then book a pickup or bring everything to the next collection day. Handover keeps selling after you've gone, and pays you wherever you are.

What if my things don't sell?

Unsold items go to local charity shops and reuse schemes, so you never have to come back for them or pay to dump them.

How much do I get?

You keep 80% of the sale price. The rest covers collection, storage and cleaning.

What can I hand over?

Kitchenware, bedding, lamps, small furniture, bikes, study things. Basically anything in a student room that you can carry. We can't take mattresses or opened food.

Is it only for international students?

No. Anyone with a college email who's moving out or moving in can use Handover. It's just especially handy when you can't take things home on a plane.
